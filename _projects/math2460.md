A browser-based arithmetic puzzle game where you spin a wheel of numbers and combine them to reach a target (24 or 60).

Built as a mobile-first single-page app with multiple color themes (Midnight, Cream, Slate, Chalk, Mint, Paper) and a clean, responsive interface.

## How it works

You're given a set of numbers and a target. Using basic arithmetic operations, combine the numbers to hit the target exactly. The spinning wheel adds a tactile, game-show feel to what's essentially a mental math challenge.
